## Capture rules

- Product must remain visually dominant; avatar should use roughly 12-15% of frame width.
- Cursor actions must visibly connect input to result. Use restrained easing and a single click ring.
- Never cover verdict labels, metric values, download control, replay controls, or Trace Court reference.
- Development evidence must be authentic and brief; no fabricated Codex UI.
- No music or decorative third-party logos.
- Final render must include a proofread English caption track.
